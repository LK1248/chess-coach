# Chess Coach

Reviews your recent Chess.com and Lichess games and explains your mistakes in plain English — no chess notation.
Phone-first and installable. Everything runs in your browser; games and results stay on your device.

- Game review: Stockfish finds the moments that changed the game; arrows show your move, the better move and their answer.
- Pattern report: your most common kinds of mistakes, with a habit to build for each and all your examples.

Run locally: `python tools/serve.py` → http://localhost:5342
